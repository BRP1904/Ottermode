Workout Prescription Glossary

What these fitness concepts mean for your lifetime physique development.

Sets

The number of times you perform a specific exercise block with standard rest pauses in between.

Reps (Repetitions)

The physical count of complete movements done inside a set without stopping (e.g., 10 squats in a row).

To Failure

Lifting with perfect safety and control until your muscles physically cannot execute another full rep.

Overload

Continuously challenging your body over months by adding heavier books or moving slower (increasing time under tension).
